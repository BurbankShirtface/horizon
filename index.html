Buying from a trusted dealer partner

When buying from a trusted dealer partner, you deal directly with a General Manager, Sales Manager, or veteran sales representative whom I know personally—often for many years. You can purchase a new or pre-owned vehicle at a fair price, simply, quickly, and transparently. There is no need for you to spend your time contacting various dealers in hopes of finding the right combination of a fair price and great client care. I ask questions to make sure the vehicle you are considering truly meets your needs now and in the future.

Selling to a trusted dealer partner

When selling a vehicle to one of my trusted dealer partners, the process is quick, hassle-free, and danger-free. Dealers buy inventory at wholesale value, so you may receive less than you would in a private sale—but there are many advantages, outlined below. The same applies if you have a vehicle you no longer need and are not replacing it: selling to a dealer partner remains a practical, low-risk option.

Why sell your vehicle to a dealer through Horizon Automotive Solutions?

1

SAVE YOUR TIME

Time is the most valuable asset you have, as you can never get more of it. You do not have to detail your vehicle, take perfect photos, or create an ad on various online portals. When you sell to a dealer, you do not have to respond to countless inquiries from strangers asking “is this available?” or wait for people who do not show up.

2

ONLY DEAL WITH REAL BUYERS

Dealers are immediate buyers, not day shoppers. They are acquiring inventory for their business, as opposed to searching for the one and only vehicle that meets their needs and budget.

3

IMMEDIATE CASH IN HAND

Dealers have the guaranteed funds available to purchase your vehicle immediately and do not have to wait for financing.

4

LESS PAPERWORK

You do not need to provide a dealer with a Used Vehicle Information Package from Service Ontario, which is a legal requirement of a private sale. They will also obtain a Carfax report. This saves you time and money.

5

THEY PAY OUT YOUR LOAN

Dealers can pay out the loan on your vehicle, or buy out the lease. When you sell your vehicle to a private buyer, the vehicle must be lien-free, requiring you to have quick and easy access to funds.

6

NO NEED TO SAFETY

Private buyers assume any vehicle being sold “as is” must have a lot of problems and expect to pay accordingly—or, more exactly, not pay. Dealers know better and will not require you to have a safety certification.

7

IT'S LEGAL

Your transaction with a dealer will be completed using a legal bill of sale that follows all provincial and federal laws. The moment you sign the contract provided by the dealer, you are absolved of further responsibilities. This keeps you safe from “curbsiders”—people who buy and sell vehicles illegally. By never registering the vehicle in their name, curbsiders shift future financial responsibility and issues to you, the last legally registered owner.

8

GET FULL MARKET EXPOSURE

Dealers will buy your vehicle at a price based on its true market value across Canada, throughout the United States, and possibly elsewhere. Private buyers will only pay what their perceived value of your car is—and only at a number that is within their budget.

9

NO POST-SALE DRAMA

Unlike many private buyers, a dealer will not contact you after they buy your car. They are not going to call you because their spouse, parents, or mechanic think they made a mistake. My trusted dealer contacts are experienced professionals; purchasing vehicles from private sellers is a business transaction for them and will always be conducted as such.

10

YOU ARE SAFE

Unfortunately, even attempting to sell your vehicle to a stranger puts you at risk of identity theft, banking scams and fraud, robbery, and physical harm. There are individuals and criminal organizations that prey on unsuspecting people who simply want to sell their car. If you choose to sell privately, please ask for my assistance to help keep you and your family away from potential danger.

Trading in with a trusted dealer

When trading in with a trusted dealer, I use live market data to help them determine the correct value for your vehicle. You do not have to negotiate or always wonder if you did the right thing. You save HST on the value of your trade-in, meaning you need to get roughly 13% more via a private sale just to break even— without taking the time and costs to sell your vehicle into consideration.

Private sale assistance

If you are set on selling your vehicle privately, I offer a service where I write your ad with the keywords that work, based on twenty-seven years of experience. I will tell you how to take pictures so your vehicle is presented in the best way. I deal with all inquiries and correspondence. I reduce some of the risk of phishing emails, scams, and fraud, but some risk remains for you. You must complete the final transaction, as only you can sell your own vehicle—but I take care of everything else. I may even have a client who is looking for what you are selling.

Flat rate for this service: $500.
